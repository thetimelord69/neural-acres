The Core Technology

Metera AI

The central nervous system of every Neural Acres facility. Not a timer. Not a thermostat. A continuously learning biological control intelligence that eliminates the parameter drift responsible for yield loss across the industry.

Metera
AI
🌡️ Temperature 18.0°C ±0.2°

Monitored continuously. Precision HVAC responds within seconds. No human operator can sustain this tolerance across a 65-day growth cycle without a single spike.

💧 Humidity 88–92% RH ±1%

Ultrasonic micro-dosing replaces manual misting. Eliminates the RH swings that dehydrate young fruiting bodies and silently reduce yield per jar.

💨 CO₂ Control <1,200 ppm

NDIR continuous sensing with motorised dampers. Maintains optimal airflow without opening the chamber — preventing contamination risks inherent in manual ventilation.

💡 LED Spectrum Dynamic PAR

Spectrum shifts dynamically from induction phase to active fruiting. Metera triggers the change at the exact biological moment — not a fixed timer schedule.

❄️ Cold Shock Induction Precisely Timed

Precisely timed fruiting induction triggered by sensor-confirmed primordia readiness signals. Increases fruiting initiation rates dramatically versus manual timing.

🔍 Anomaly Detection <1% Target

Thermal and atmospheric anomalies detected within hours via sensor fusion. Industry contamination average is 5–10%. Metera targets below 1% — meaning more of every cycle is saleable.

Why Kerala for Phase 1: Kerala's industrial ecosystem offers the precise combination Neural Acres needs at launch — available controlled-environment industrial space, a growing pool of microbiological and AI engineering talent, proximity to the KSUM venture network, and strong state government support for agri-biotech ventures.

The Phase 1 site selection prioritises capital efficiency, ensuring the largest proportion of the initial raise funds the Metera AI platform and production hardware — the actual competitive moat — rather than premium real estate. Facility location will be confirmed and announced upon seed funding close.

As Phase 2 botanical production scales and export volumes grow, Neural Acres will evaluate additional facility locations across Kerala and Tamil Nadu to support the broader expansion roadmap.
